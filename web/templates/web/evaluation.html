Reading the table

  • nDCG, precision and hit rate measure how many of the reference tracks (Last.fm similar tracks) appear in the top k. They need reference data. Values are the mean over seed tracks with a 95% bootstrap interval underneath.
  • Coverage is the share of the catalogue that appears in at least one list. Low coverage means the same songs keep coming back.
  • Diversity is one minus the average similarity between songs in a list. Higher means more varied lists.
  • Novelty rises when lists lean on less popular songs. It needs listener counts from Last.fm.
  • ms is the average time to build one list, measured on this machine.

The reference data comes from listening behaviour, not from genre, mood, tempo or decade, so it is a fair outside check but not a perfect one. It rewards songs many people play together, which favours popular tracks.
